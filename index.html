GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions

Is the library open 24 hours?

Yes, Mr. King Library is open 24 hours. Please confirm your assigned shift with management.

What is the night shift fee?

The night shift runs from 9:00 PM to 6:00 AM and costs ₹300 per month. Confirm seat and room allocation with management.

Are AC and Non-AC plans available?

Yes. Both options are available with different assigned study durations. See the complete monthly fee table above.

Is there a separate hall for girls?

Yes, a separate study hall is available for girls.

Is student parking available?

Yes. A dedicated on-site parking area is available for students under CCTV surveillance.

Can I pay at the end of the month?

No. Fees are payable in advance; month-end payment is not available. Fees once paid are non-refundable.

Does scanning the payment QR confirm my fee automatically?

No. The QR is for manual UPI payment. Please confirm the recipient and coordinate with library management for payment confirmation. Never share your UPI PIN or OTP.

Where is the library located?

NH-139, opposite Kali Mandir, Walidad Masjid Gali, Walidad, Bihar. Use the Google Maps link above for directions.
